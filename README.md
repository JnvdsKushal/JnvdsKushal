## About Me

I'm a student and developer who enjoys building technology that solves practical, real-world problems. I care about understanding complete systems — from problem definition and architecture to integrations and deployment — rather than building things just to try out a tool.

I like combining different technologies with a clear purpose, and I'm constantly experimenting, learning, and improving through what I build. What motivates me most is taking an idea and turning it into something functional and capable of solving an actual problem.
